NFS Load Mini deployed.

1) Get service:
   kubectl get svc -l app.kubernetes.io/name=nfs-load-mini

2) Port-forward:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "nfs-load-mini.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

3) Open:
   http://localhost:8080

Mounted NFS paths inside pod:
- Private: {{ .Values.nfs.private.mountPath }}
- Public : {{ .Values.nfs.public.mountPath }}
